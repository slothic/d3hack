D3Hack -- Greater / Nephalem Rift map list
==============================================================================

164 maps in 62 families.

Turn on MapNameOverlay and the game names each floor's map on screen as you
enter it. When one annoys you, find it here and add its internal name to:

    [rare_cheats]
    BannedRiftMaps = "x1_lr_tileset_hexmaze, x1_lr_tileset_sewers"

Each SNO bans separately, so you can ban small Graveyard and keep the large one.
A name that matches nothing is reported in the log, so typos are visible.

SHAPE is the column that usually decides it:
    OPEN      wide, easy to group packs and cover ground
    open      reasonably open
    corridor  hallways, single-file pulls
    cave      tight winding tunnels, the worst for grouping
    MAZE      actively maze-like, easy to get lost in

The in-game names are identified from the asset names and the zones they come
from -- they are a guide, not an official table. The overlay is the authority:
if a floor looks nothing like the description here, trust your eyes.
